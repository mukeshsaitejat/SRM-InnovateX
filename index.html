Pick a track, pick a problem, and ship something real by sunrise — with checkpoints, mentors and prizes along the way.

FREQUENTLY
ASKED

SRM INNOVATEX is a student-driven hackathon open to students who form teams to build a technology solution over the 24-hour window.
Teams of 3 to 5 members, with registration fees of ₹1,399, ₹1,799 and ₹2,099 respectively.
The core product-building window is 24 hours. The overall event — including orientation, evaluations and final presentations — may extend by up to approximately 6 additional hours.
Open Innovation, FinTech, Healthcare, AgriTech, Sustainability, IoT/Hardware and EduTech — teams pick one problem statement within their chosen track.
An initial pitch within the first 20 minutes, followed by checkpoint evaluations at roughly +7 and +15 hours, and a final evaluation of shortlisted projects by judges and industry professionals.
Dinner is currently planned. Breakfast, lunch and additional refreshments are being arranged but are not confirmed yet.
A laptop, charger, an extension board, a power bank if needed, and any personal essentials for a 24-hour build.
